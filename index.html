FAQ

Common questions

How long does a typical website take?

A Starter site usually takes 1–2 weeks. E-commerce or multi-language builds run 3–6 weeks depending on scope, confirmed after your free quote.

Do you work with clients outside Ghana?

Yes — we currently support clients in Ghana, Kazakhstan, the Netherlands, and the United States, and we're set up to work with any timezone.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. Every package includes a maintenance option, so you have a direct line to us for updates rather than starting over with someone new each time.

Can you help even if I already have a website?

Yes — redesigns are one of our most common projects. We can also just take over DNS, email, or SEO without rebuilding the whole site.
